How the 5×7 matrix reaches Level 7

Each participant invites five qualifying people, and each of those people does the same, creating a theoretical seven-level structure. With ₦10,000 qualifying monthly PQV per active participant, the 15% allocation is ₦1,500 per participant. Applying the current descending level rates to a fully populated matrix projects approximately ₦12.86 million in combined monthly payouts across all seven levels.

The approximately ₦12.86 million figure is the sum of the illustrative per-position payouts across all seven levels when every position is filled and qualifies at ₦10,000 monthly PQV. Real participation, eligibility, purchase volume, timing, adjustments and the approved compensation plan can change outcomes. The ₦15 million Level 7 figure is a separate programme target/modelled opportunity and must not be interpreted as a guaranteed payment to every member.

Before you join

Frequently asked questions

Is this only an investment scheme?

No. It is presented as a food and agribusiness participation ecosystem involving learning, production, products and community activity. Do not join on the assumption of guaranteed financial returns.

Are referral or matrix rewards guaranteed?

No. They depend on qualifying activity, eligibility, actual network participation and the official compensation plan. Figures on this page are illustrations, not promises of income.

What are Green Card Points?

Programme points intended for designated production and consumption purposes. They are not cash; usage and unlock conditions are governed by the published rules.

How do I join a local group?

Use the WhatsApp link on this page for onboarding guidance and information about your local community group.

Are farm slots and products available everywhere?

Availability can vary by location, season and project capacity. Confirm availability, delivery, terms and timelines before paying.
